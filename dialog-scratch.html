Confirmation Dialog

Three approaches to replacing the browser's native confirm() dialog with something that feels like part of the News Builder. Each uses the same prompt: "Remove this card?"

Option A
Centered Modal
A focused overlay that pauses the interface. The scrim draws attention to the decision. Best for destructive actions that warrant a moment of pause.
Lead Story Card
Breaking News Bar
Supporting Story Card
Option B
Anchored Popover
A compact confirmation that drops from the Remove button itself. No scrim — the rest of the interface stays visible. Fast and contextual. Works well when the action is easy to undo or lower-stakes.
Lead Story Card
Breaking News Bar

Option C
Inline Takeover
The confirmation expands directly within the card row — no overlay, no popover. The card reveals a tinted strip asking for confirmation. Keeps the user oriented in context.
